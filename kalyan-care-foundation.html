Who we are

Kalyan Care Foundation is a registered non-profit working on the ground with a simple belief: care doesn't need a grand occasion. A birthday, an ordinary evening, an empty bowl outside a shop — any of these is reason enough to show up. We run small, consistent drives that turn ordinary moments into meals, and we share every one of them so you can see exactly where your support goes.

Three ways we show up

Each one started as a single act, and became something we now do regularly.

Birthday Meals

On someone's birthday, we deliver home-style meals to people who need them most, along with a personal birthday message. It turns one person's celebration into another person's good day — something we've shared often enough now that it's become one of our most-watched reels.

Feed a Soul

Street dogs get hungry too. Feed a Soul is our ongoing drive to put food in front of the strays in our neighbourhoods — regularly, and without waiting to be asked. ₹35 is what it takes to feed one for a day.

Donate in Someone's Name

Mark a birthday, an anniversary, or the memory of someone you love by dedicating a donation in their name. We carry it out and let you know it reached someone.
